Our Philosophy

More Than A Watch.
It's A Legacy.

At Overtime NYC, fine watchmaking is approached with genuine passion, discretion, and respect for every mechanical detail. Located in the heart of New York City's historic 47th Street district, our showroom serves collectors, enthusiasts, and first-time buyers seeking an elevated, personal experience.

Whether acquiring a milestone reference, exploring a trade, or entrusting a prized timepiece for professional repair, refinishing, or in-person authentication, every client receives dedicated, one-on-one attention tailored to their goals.

We believe the experience of acquiring and caring for a luxury watch should be as enduring as the timepiece itself.
